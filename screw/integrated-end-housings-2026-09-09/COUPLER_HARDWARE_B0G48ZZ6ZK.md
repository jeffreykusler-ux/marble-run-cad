Design consequence:
The previous conservative 16 mm x 24 mm coupler clearance envelope can be reduced. Keep at least 1.0 mm radial clearance around the 15 mm coupler, so the production free-rotation envelope should be >=17 mm diameter. Axial service envelope should allow the 20 mm body plus assembly/disengagement clearance.

Do not design any printed part to touch or radially support the flexible coupler. It must rotate freely and transmit torque only.
