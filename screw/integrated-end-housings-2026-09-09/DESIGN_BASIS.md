# Integrated Screw End Housings — engineering candidate

Date: 2026-09-09

Status: **NOT PRINT AUTHORIZED**

This branch develops a new precision support architecture without replacing Rev T by implication.

## Mechanical architecture

The shaft and two stationary guide rods are controlled by a common three-axis yoke at the lower and upper ends. Two R4 bearings support the rotor independently of the N20/flexible coupler.

Lower axial load path:

A01/A03 -> thin washer contacting only R4 inner race -> R4 -> bearing pocket -> lower tower/plinth -> wood base.

The motor and coupler are below the lower bearing and transmit torque only. The two shafts must not bottom inside the coupler.

## Carried-forward measurements

- shaft and guide rods: 6.35 mm
- R4 measured OD: 15.86 mm
- R4 measured width: 4.99 mm
- corrected new-revision pocket: 15.96 mm diameter x 4.90 mm depth
- current F02 cap mounting pitch: 30.00 mm
- R01 mesh minimum center-opening radius: approximately 44.92 mm

## Guide radius

The final guide radius remains physical-fit controlled. The current G02 mesh suggests approximately +/-33.25 mm as a nominal working location, but the yoke does not permanently freeze it. Replaceable guide inserts provide -1.50, -0.75, 0, +0.75 and +1.50 mm bore offsets so the large housings do not need reprinting after calibration.
